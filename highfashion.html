Tamiu Abdualrahman Kayode, born on March 18, 1990 is a prominent Nigerian Celebrity widely known as Rahman Jago.
Rahman Jago is a Nigerian music promoter, Influencer and Enterpreneur known for his strong influence in the Nigerian street-pop
scene. He rose to Prominence through his close association with key figures in the Zanku movement, practicularly artist like Zlatan
Ibile and Naria Marley. Born and raised in Ngeria, Rahman Jago latr pent significant time in dubia, which became a major part of his
public identity and lifestyle branding. Through not primarily known as a mainstream recording artist, he has relased music
collaboration, and appered in several street anthem that gain popularity within Nigeria's urban music culture. He became widely recongnized during the late 2010s as the Zanku sound.
The street-hop movement gained momentum.Rather than positioning himself stictly as a singer, Rahman Jago built his reputation as a cultural figure someone deeply connected to nightlife, youth culture, fashion and luxury living. His social media presence, especially showcasing

overtime, he evolved into more than just an associate of major artist.
